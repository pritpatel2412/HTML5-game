Game Modes Guide

Endless vs Zen vs Daily Challenge: Which Mode Should You Play?

Whether you want high-stakes roguelite progression, fair competitive seeded runs, or pure stress-free mindfulness.

Published by Stardust Merge Arcade Studio • Updated November 2026 • 5 min read
✨ Quick Summary: The 3 Stardust Merge Modes

Stardust Merge features three modes catering to different moods: 1. Endless Odyssey provides the core roguelite arcade experience with unlocked Cosmic Perks and high scores. 2. Daily Challenge uses a globally synchronized date seed (mulberry32) giving all players worldwide the identical sequence of orbs for competitive parity. 3. Zen Nebula removes the Event Horizon game-over barrier completely, allowing unlimited pressure-free cosmic stacking.

2. Endless Odyssey: The Definitive Experience

If you want the complete Stardust Merge journey, Endless Odyssey is your go-to mode.

Here, you drop orbs with standard arcade physics, chase personal high scores, trigger multi-tier chain combos, and draft stackable Cosmic Perks as you cross 1,000, 3,000, and 7,500 points. If you manage to fuse two Black Holes into a Supernova Ascension, your run continues endlessly with multiplied scoring!

3. Daily Challenge: 100% Fair Competition

In many puzzle games, high scores are heavily influenced by lucky random drops. Stardust Merge eliminates luck through the Daily Challenge:

  • Every midnight UTC, the game initializes a deterministic mulberry32 pseudo-random number generator seeded with the current calendar date (YYYYMMDD).
  • Every player in the world receives the exact same sequence of upcoming orbs.
  • Your final score reflects pure skill, spatial foresight, and combo precision!

4. Zen Nebula: Mindful Cosmic Meditation

Sometimes you just want to unwind after a long day without the stress of alarms, flashing timers, or game-over screens.

Zen Nebula mode deactivates the Event Horizon entirely. Orbs can stack as high as you like. You can experiment with physics, listen to soothing procedural chimes, and admire the beautiful cosmic colors without fear of losing your run.

💡 How to Switch Modes Instantly

You can switch modes at any time directly inside the in-game header tabs or by launching the game with mode anchors (such as /HTML5-game/#daily or /HTML5-game/#zen). Your settings, high scores, and unlocked themes are preserved independently for each mode!
