2. Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Sound is not playing on my phone. How do I fix it?

Most modern mobile browsers (especially iOS Safari and Android Chrome) require a user gesture (a tap or swipe) before allowing the Web Audio API to output sound. Tap anywhere on the game screen or check the Mute icon in the top header.

Did I lose my progress if I clear my browser cookies?

Yes. Because Stardust Merge operates on a zero-account, privacy-first architecture, high scores and cosmetics are saved inside your device's browser localStorage. Clearing your browser's site data will reset your local profile.

Can I embed Stardust Merge on my gaming portal?

We welcome links to Stardust Merge! If you operate an indie gaming portal or school-friendly gaming hub, please reach out via email to discuss iframe embedding and attribution guidelines.
