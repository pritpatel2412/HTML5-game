How to Play Stardust Merge: Complete Beginner's Guide

Everything you need to know about dropping orbs, chaining kinetic fusions, managing your jar, and surviving past the Event Horizon.

Published by Stardust Merge Arcade Studio • Updated November 2026 • 5 min read
⚡ Quick Summary: How Do You Play Stardust Merge?

In Stardust Merge, you position and drop glowing celestial bodies into a glass jar. When two orbs of the identical tier touch, they fuse into a single larger body of the next tier, awarding score points and combo energy. Your goal is to synthesize your way through 11 astronomical tiers from microscopic Dust to supermassive Black Holes without allowing your stack to overflow past the top Event Horizon danger line.

1. The Core Objective: The Glass Jar Ecosystem

Unlike grid-based matching games where tiles snap to fixed squares, Stardust Merge runs on a continuous 2D rigid-body physics engine (Matter.js). Every celestial orb has weight, surface bounce, momentum, and friction.

When dropped, orbs roll off curved surfaces, wedge into crevices, and settle naturally under gravity. Your mission is to continuously combine pairs of matching bodies so the containment jar never becomes overwhelmed.

2. Controls: Aiming & Dropping

Stardust Merge supports three distinct control schemes engineered for seamless play across every device:

Device Aim Movement Drop Action Fine Nudge
Desktop Mouse Move mouse horizontally across the top container rim Left-click or release mouse button Screen arrows or Q / E keys
Mobile Touch Drag finger along the top guidance rail Lift finger or tap the bottom "DROP" capsule Left/Right bottom thumb nudge buttons
Keyboard ← / → Arrow keys or A / D Space, Enter, or ↓ Arrow Q (left nudge) / E (right nudge)
💡 Laser Guidance Line

When aiming, notice the translucent laser guide and ghost preview orb. It projects the exact drop trajectory and shows the true physical radius of your incoming celestial body before you commit to dropping it.

3. Orb Fusion & Evolution Hierarchy

Merging is the heartbeat of Stardust Merge. Whenever two orbs of the exact same tier make physical contact:

  1. A localized gravitational force pulls both bodies toward their collective midpoint.
  2. Both parent orbs vanish in a sparkling flash of cosmic dust and starlight.
  3. The next tier body spawns instantly at that exact position with realistic kinetic momentum.
  4. Points are credited to your active run score, multiplied by your current combo tier.

Review our detailed breakdown of all 11 celestial bodies in the Complete 11 Cosmic Orb Tiers Guide.

4. The Hold & Swap Slot

In the top-left HUD sits the HOLD capsule. If you receive an orb that cannot immediately merge with anything in your jar (such as a large Asteroid when your bottom layer is composed of small Sparks), tap the Hold button or press H on your keyboard.

  • If the hold slot is empty, your current orb is placed into reserve and the next queued orb drops into your launcher.
  • If the hold slot already holds an orb, pressing Hold swaps your active orb with the stored one.
  • You can swap once per turn. Once you drop an orb, the hold slot refreshes for your next drop.

5. The Event Horizon Danger Line

Stretched across the upper boundary of the glass jar is a dashed red danger line known as the Event Horizon.

If any orb settles or gets pushed above this line, a red containment warning flashes and a countdown timer starts:

  • Base Grace Window: 2.5 continuous seconds above the line causes containment collapse (Game Over).
  • Slow Orbit Perk: Unlocking the Slow Orbit Cosmic Perk increases this safety buffer to 4.0 seconds, giving you valuable extra time for cascade merges to pull orbs downward.
🌟 What Happens When You Merge Two Black Holes?

Merging two Tier-11 Black Holes achieves the legendary Supernova Ascension! The jar detonates in a majestic slow-motion cosmic wave, vaporizing all orbs, granting 5,000 bonus points, and permanently awarding a ×1.5 score multiplier for the remainder of your run. Learn more in our Supernova Ascension Guide.

6. Unlocking Roguelite Cosmic Perks

As your score crosses major cosmic milestones (such as 1,000, 3,000, and 7,500 points), the game pauses to present a choice of three random Cosmic Perks:

  • Attractor Pulse: Matching orbs pull toward each other across small gaps when stationary.
  • Shockwave: Merges emit a kinetic shockwave that gently dislodges stuck neighboring orbs.
  • Combo Keeper: Extends your combo window from 1.2s to 2.2s, making ×8 multipliers much easier to sustain.
  • Star Bomb Charge: Powers up your emergency Star Bomb, clearing tiny trapped Dust and Spark orbs from the bottom of your container.
