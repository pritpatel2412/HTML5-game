Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Summary in Plain English: Stardust Merge is a static client-side web game. We do not require accounts, we do not collect your name or password, and all game state (scores, high combos, unlocked cosmetics, and settings) lives directly in your own browser's local storage.

1. What Data is Stored on Your Device

When you play Stardust Merge, the game stores data locally on your device using client-side web technologies:

  • localStorage & IndexedDB: Used to save your gameplay progress, high score, coins earned, perk unlocks, active missions, custom settings (volume, haptics, color-blind mode), and consent flags. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export your save code.
  • Service Worker Cache: Pre-caches application code, SVG icons, fonts, and game assets so you can play offline in airplane mode and enjoy instant page loading.

2. No Account Creation or Personal Data Collection

We do not operate backend user accounts. We do not require you to register, sign in, or provide personal details such as your phone number, social security number, or home address. You play as an anonymous celestial voyager.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

To keep Stardust Merge free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners.

  • Google AdSense: Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads: Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalized (tailored to your interests using cookies and advertising identifiers) or non-personalized (contextual ads based on page content and approximate location).
  • Opt-Out Options: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can also change your preferences anytime in our in-game .

4. Optional Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with IP anonymization enabled to track high-level gameplay metrics (such as highest tier reached, run durations, and game mode popularity). Analytics scripts are loaded only after explicit consent is granted via our consent banner. You may revoke consent at any time.

5. How to Delete Your Local Data

You have full sovereign control over your data. To permanently erase all saved progress, scores, and preferences:

  1. Open the game and click the Settings (Gear) icon.
  2. Scroll down to the Data Management section.
  3. Tap Reset Progress (executing resetLocalData) and confirm the prompt. All local storage entries will be wiped immediately.
  4. Alternatively, you can clear website data for this site via your browser's Privacy & Security settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Stardust Merge is a general audience casual puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. GDPR, UK & CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to specific rights under data protection laws:

  • Right to Know / Access: Since we do not store personal profiles on our servers, all your data is stored locally in your browser.
  • Right to Deletion: You can purge your data at any time via the in-game Settings menu or browser settings.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale / Sharing: We do not sell personal data. You can disable interest-based advertising via our cookie preferences banner.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will receive full access to gameplay regardless of your privacy choices.
