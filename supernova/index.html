How the Supernova Mechanic Works in Stardust Merge

The climax of cosmic evolution: uniting two Tier-11 Black Holes into a screen-clearing Supernova Ascension.

Published by Stardust Merge Arcade Studio • Updated November 2026 • 5 min read
💥 Quick Summary: What Is a Supernova Ascension?

In Stardust Merge, a Supernova Ascension is triggered when two Tier-11 Black Holes touch. Unlike conventional merge games where reaching the highest tier ends the game or does nothing, a Supernova triggers a slow-motion shockwave that clears the entire containment jar, awards 5,000 bonus points, and permanently grants a ×1.5 score multiplier for the rest of your run.

1. The Trigger Condition

Reaching a single Tier-11 Black Hole requires synthesizing two Tier-10 Neutron Stars, four Tier-9 Suns, and hundreds of base drops.

In Stardust Merge, the evolution does not stop there. Once your first Black Hole rests at the bottom of the jar, your ultimate goal is to craft a second Black Hole and let their accretion disks make physical contact.

2. The 4-Phase Ascension Sequence

When the two singularities collide, the game executes a cinematic 4-phase sequence:

  1. Singularity Dilation: The Matter.js physics engine enters slow-motion mode, reducing simulation speed by 50% for 1,200ms while dynamic canvas bloom radiates outward.
  2. Radiant Shockwave: A blinding ring of starlight expands from the contact point, gently dissolving every other orb currently inside the jar into glittering stardust particles.
  3. Stardust Score Bounty: An instant flat bonus of +5,000 points is credited to your run total.
  4. Permanent ×1.5 Multiplier: Your ascension multiplier permanently increments by +0.5 (e.g. ×1.0 becomes ×1.5, a second supernova yields ×2.0). Every subsequent merge earns points multiplied by this new factor!
🏆 Endless Rebirth, Zero Forced Game-Over

In many classic physics merge games, crafting the largest object leaves you with a giant immovable sphere that blocks the container until you inevitably lose. Stardust Merge solves this by treating the pinnacle of evolution as a rewarding reset that lets true masters keep playing indefinitely!

3. Proven Tactics for Building Twin Black Holes

Creating two Black Holes simultaneously within the confines of the glass jar requires disciplined spatial management:

  • Pin the First Singularity: As soon as your first Black Hole forms, use small orbs to nudge it into the far left corner.
  • Dedicate the Right Flank: Build your second progression ladder strictly on the right half of the container.
  • Avoid Mid-Tier Barriers: Do not let a Sun or Ocean World get wedged between the two Black Holes. Keep the central floor clean so the second Black Hole rolls smoothly into the first.
