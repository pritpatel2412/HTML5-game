Cosmic Perk Tier List: Ranking Every Stacking Buff

A competitive breakdown of all 8 milestone perks to help you build unstoppable celestial synergy.

1. How Cosmic Perks Scale in Endless Mode

In Endless Mode, crossing score milestones triggers the Mochi Milestone Perk Picker. The first perk arrives at 450 points, and subsequent milestones scale dynamically based on the formula: Math.round(450 × Math.pow(1.45, stepCount)).

Because each run grants a finite number of perk opportunities, selecting the right buff can make the difference between capping out at 20,000 points or sailing into multi-Supernova territory above 150,000.

2. S-Tier: Essential Game-Winners

1. PILLOW LANDING (soft_landing)

Effect: Cushions plush impacts (-45% bounce restitution) for calmer, steadier stacking.

Why it's S-Tier: High-tier celestial bodies (especially Sunny and Neutry) carry tremendous mass in Matter.js. Without Pillow Landing, dropping an orb into a crowded jar creates violent kinetic shocks that bounce adjacent bodies over the Event Horizon. Pillow Landing eliminates runaway ricochets completely.

2. COMBO RIBBON (combo_keeper)

Effect: Extends the active merge combo window by +1.0 second per stack.

Why it's S-Tier: The default 1,200ms window requires simultaneous cascades. Adding +1,000ms per stack enables manual chain drops. With 2 stacks (3.2s window), you can maintain maximum 8x multipliers continuously across your entire run.

3. A-Tier: High Value & Consistency

3. GOLDEN DUSTY (golden_dust)

Effect: Tier-1 Dusty drops have a 35% chance per stack to upgrade automatically into Tier-2 Sparky.

Why it's A-Tier: Dusty represents 42% of all natural drops. Upgrading over a third of them to Sparky bypasses the lowest, most clutter-prone tier, drastically reducing dead jar space and saving dozens of moves.

4. GRAVITY CUDDLE (gravity_well)

Effect: Dropped creatures gently pull matching-tier friends together for 1.0 second upon landing.

Why it's A-Tier: This perk acts as a magnetic guide, pulling matching orbs around obstacles even when your aim is slightly off-target or blocked by uneven terrain.

4. B-Tier: Solid Tactical Utility

5. SPARKLE CHAIN (chain_reaction)

Effect: 25% chance per stack that a merge pulls nearby matching creatures with +50% bonus points.

Verdict: Phenomenal for bonus score generation, but slightly RNG-dependent. When it procs on high-tier merges, it generates tens of thousands of instant points.

6. SAFE BUBBLE (slow_orbit)

Effect: Adds +1.5 seconds of Event Horizon grace time before game over triggers.

Verdict: Increases your danger timer from 2.5s to 4.0s (or 5.5s at stack 2). A great insurance policy for players who tend to cut it close to the red line.

5. C-Tier: Situational Picks

7. STAR POP +1 (star_bomb)

Effect: Grants +1 immediate Star Bomb charge to pop the 5 smallest orbs for bonus points.

Verdict: Strong emergency relief, but a one-time charge is generally less valuable than permanent passive stacking buffs like Pillow Landing or Combo Ribbon.

8. MOCHI PEEK (lucky_next)

Effect: Reveals 2 upcoming creatures in the Next preview window and boosts high-tier drop odds.

Verdict: Helpful for foresight and planning, but high-skill players already manage the single Next preview well enough using the Hold pocket.
