How to Reach Coco the Black Hole & Supernova

A strategic blueprint for surviving late-game density bottlenecks and triggering endless Supernova Ascensions.

1. The Late-Game Space Problem

Building Tier 1 through Tier 7 in Stardust Merge is relatively forgiving. Up to Bubbly (Tier 7, radius 60px), you have ample room in the 400px wide jar to maneuver multiple bodies side-by-side.

However, the mathematical reality of rigid circles shifts dramatically from Tier 8 onward:

  • Tier 8 (Saturny): Radius 71px (Diameter 142px). Two Saturny orbs occupy 284px—over 70% of the jar's logical width!
  • Tier 9 (Sunny): Radius 83px (Diameter 166px). Two Sun orbs take 332px, leaving only 68px of wiggle room.
  • Tier 10 (Neutry): Radius 96px (Diameter 192px). Two Neutron stars measure 384px across—virtually the entire 400px jar!
  • Tier 11 (Coco): Radius 110px (Diameter 220px). Two Black Holes cannot fit side-by-side horizontally (220 + 220 = 440px > 400px). They must settle at a diagonal angle!

2. The Ladder Hierarchy and Points Breakdown

Every time you reach a new milestone tier, the points awarded scale quadratically:

Merged Tier Creature Name Radius Base Points Earned
8 Saturny (Ringed Giant) 71 px 640 pts
9 Sunny (Sun) 83 px 810 pts
10 Neutry (Neutron Star) 96 px 1,000 pts
11 Coco (Black Hole) 110 px 1,210 pts
SUPERNOVA 2 × Coco Fusion Clear Jar 5,000 pts × Ascension

3. The Anchor Strategy for Coco

To successfully create Coco the Black Hole, you must commit to an Uncompromising Anchor Corner from the opening minute of your run:

Rule: Never let a high-tier orb wander to the center.

Pick the bottom-left corner. Build your first Sunny (Tier 9) or Neutry (Tier 10) directly into that wall. Once anchored, all intermediate tiers (5, 6, 7, 8) must form an ascending ramp sloping upward toward the right wall.

4. Solving the “Separated Twins” Crisis

The most common run-killer occurs when a Tier 9 Sunny settles in the left corner, and a second Sunny accidentally spawns or settles against the right wall, separated by a stack of smaller orbs.

If this happens, do not panic! You have three tactical options:

  1. The Bridge Merge: Build smaller orbs in the middle canyon up to Tier 8 Saturny. As the intermediate orb fuses, the physics engine will violently compress the gap and draw the two Sun bodies together.
  2. Deploy the Shake Power-Up: Press the Shake button to deliver a sudden impulse jolt. This causes spherical bodies to roll over uneven peaks and slide together.
  3. Star Bomb Purge: Fire a Star Bomb to instantly disintegrate the 5 smallest orbs in the jar. This evaporates the debris holding the two titans apart, allowing gravity to bring them into contact.

5. Triggering the Supernova Ascension

When you finally bring two Tier-11 Black Holes into physical contact, the game triggers a Supernova Ascension:

  • A blinding celebratory flash envelopes the jar.
  • Every single remaining orb inside the jar is completely vaporized, resetting your playing field to a clean slate.
  • You are awarded a flat 5,000 base points multiplied by your current Ascension rank.
  • Your global score multiplier permanently climbs by 1.5x (e.g. 1.0x → 1.5x → 2.25x → 3.38x).
  • Mochi transforms into excited celebration mode!

Because Supernova wipes the board clean while compounding your score multiplier, runs that achieve multiple Supernovas reach astronomical scores in the hundreds of thousands!
