Deep Dive into Combos, Cascade Timing & Multipliers

The exact mathematical engine that transforms ordinary merges into world-record scores.

1. The Architecture of the Score Formula

In Stardust Merge, points are not assigned arbitrarily. The game engine executes an exact mathematical formula whenever two orbs fuse:

Points = Math.round(Tier × Tier × 10 × Combo × AscensionMult)

Notice the quadratic term: Tier × Tier. A Tier-2 Sparky awards 2 × 2 × 10 = 40 pts, but a Tier-10 Neutry awards 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000 pts. That is 25 times more points before any multipliers are applied!

2. The 1,200ms Combo Window

Whenever any merge occurs in the jar, the game engine sets an internal countdown timer: BASE_COMBO_WINDOW_MS = 1,200 milliseconds (1.2 seconds).

If a second merge occurs before those 1,200ms elapse:

  • The combo counter increases by +1 (e.g., from 1x to 2x, then 3x).
  • The timer resets to full duration (plus any active perk bonuses).
  • Visual fireworks flash and Web Audio synthesis raises the pitch of the chime harmonic.
  • The combo multiplier caps out at MAX_COMBO = 8.

At maximum combo (8x), a Tier-10 Neutry merge explodes with 8,000 points in a single pop!

3. The Power of the Combo Ribbon Perk

Because the base window is only 1.2 seconds, spontaneous cascades are often required to maintain long combo streaks. That is why the Combo Ribbon perk is widely regarded as one of the strongest buffs in the game:

Combo Ribbon Math:

Each stack of Combo Ribbon adds +1,000ms (1.0 second) to your combo window. With two stacks, your window grows from 1.2s to 3.2 full seconds! At 3.2 seconds, you have enough time to manually aim and drop new orbs to keep an 8x combo running indefinitely.

4. Engineering Gravity Cascades

A “cascade” happens when one merge collapses support beneath other bodies, causing them to tumble downward into secondary collisions. You can proactively engineer cascades with careful board staging:

  1. Step Staging: Position a pair of Tier 3 Commies directly above a pair of Tier 4 Rockies. When the Commies merge into a Rocky, the newly created Rocky drops directly into the waiting Rocky below for an automatic instant 2x combo!
  2. The Sparkle Chain Bonus: With the Sparkle Chain perk, every merge has a 25% chance per stack to trigger gravitational attraction on nearby identical orbs. If this triggers, adjacent orbs are sucked in and grant an additional +50% bonus points (Math.round(basePoints × 0.5))!

5. Ascension Compounding: The True High Score Engine

The final multiplier term in the formula is AscensionMult. Every time two Black Holes merge to create a Supernova:

  • Ascension 0 (Start): Mult = 1.00x
  • Ascension 1 (1st Supernova): Mult = 1.50x
  • Ascension 2 (2nd Supernova): Mult = 2.25x
  • Ascension 3 (3rd Supernova): Mult = 3.38x
  • Ascension 4 (4th Supernova): Mult = 5.06x

At Ascension 4 and 8x combo, a single Tier 11 Coco merge produces:
1,210 × 8 × 5.06 = 48,980 points!
