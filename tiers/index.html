🌌 Quick Summary: What Are the 11 Orb Tiers in Stardust Merge?

Stardust Merge features 11 astronomical tiers progressing exponentially in size and mass: 1. Dust, 2. Spark, 3. Comet, 4. Asteroid, 5. Moon, 6. Ember, 7. Ocean World, 8. Ringed Giant, 9. Sun, 10. Neutron Star, and 11. Black Hole. Only Tiers 1–4 spawn naturally from the top launcher; all higher tiers (5–11) must be synthesized through merging.

2. Spawn Probabilities & Launcher Rules

To maintain fair and strategic gameplay, the drop queue only generates the first four tiers:

  • Tier 1 (Dust): 42% chance — Ideal for fine-tuning combos and filling minor gaps.
  • Tier 2 (Spark): 30% chance — The primary building block for early cascades.
  • Tier 3 (Comet): 19% chance — Medium body requiring deliberate placement.
  • Tier 4 (Asteroid): 9% chance — The largest direct spawn; use the Hold slot if awkward!

Because Tiers 5 through 11 can never be dropped from the launcher, synthesizing them requires deliberate chain planning.

3. Colorblind Accessibility & Geometric Cues

Stardust Merge was designed from day one to be fully accessible for players with deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia:

  • Geometric Silhouette Patterns: Each orb tier draws a distinct procedural surface pattern (such as diamond sparkles, craters, wavy rings, or pulsar beams) directly inside its Canvas rendering routine.
  • High-Contrast Numeral Overlay: In the in-game Settings menu, players can toggle on numeral overlays (1 through 11) for instant numerical identification.
  • Rim Illumination: High-luminance rim strokes clearly define the outer collision boundary against the dark space background.

4. Mass & Physics Integration

In Matter.js, body mass scales cubically with radius:

A Tier-1 Dust orb (radius 13px) has a negligible physical mass, meaning it is easily bounced aside or launched airborne by heavy collisions. Conversely, a Tier-9 Sun (radius 83px) or Tier-11 Black Hole (radius 110px) acts as an immovable gravitational anchor that small orbs slide over.

💡 Strategic Implication

Never place small orbs underneath high-tier bodies. The tremendous physical weight of a Sun or Ringed Giant will compress small orbs against the bottom of the container, making them nearly impossible to dislodge without a Star Bomb!
