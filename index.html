Welcome to the Kawaii Cosmic Fluff Universe!

Stardust Merge is a cozy kawaii physics puzzle game starring Mochi—an irresistibly fluffy cloud-astronaut mascot wearing a transparent bubble helmet, a purple scarf, and a golden star badge—and a collectible family of 11 living cosmic creatures. Inside a floating frosted glass jar, every drop brings two identical plush friends together so they can cuddle and evolve into the next astronomical character on the ladder!

Every character in Stardust Merge has its own distinct silhouette, plush texture, and living animations: from shy little Dusty (Tier 1) and hyperactive Sparky (Tier 2) to sleepy Luna (Tier 5), cheerful Sunny (Tier 9), and the legendary black-hole plush Coco (Tier 11). When two Coco characters merge, their accretion rings spin up in excitement and unleash a dazzling Magical Supernova with Mochi!

How to Play & Pro Strategy Tips

  • Aim with Mochi & Drop: Drag horizontally to float Mochi across the jar rim, or use Left / Right arrow keys and Space to drop.
  • Keep Heavy Friends Anchored: Park Sunny, Neutry, and Coco on one side of the jar so smaller creatures roll smoothly down into combo chains.
  • Use Hold & Star Bombs Wisely: Swap an inconvenient orb into the Hold bubble, or trigger a Star Bomb to pop the 5 smallest creatures when they block a big merge.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Stardust Merge require a download or sign-in?

Never! Stardust Merge opens and plays in under 2 seconds right in your browser with zero accounts or downloads required.

What happens when two Coco (Black Hole) characters merge?

Merging two Tier-11 Coco characters triggers a Magical Supernova—both characters spin their rings rapidly, their eyes widen in excitement, a giant star flashes across the screen, Mochi celebrates, the jar clears for 5,000+ bonus points, and your run multiplier increases by ×1.5!
