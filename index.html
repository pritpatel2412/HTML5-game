⚡ Quick Summary: What Is Stardust Merge?

Stardust Merge is a free, browser-based physics merge puzzle game where players drop glowing cosmic bodies into a floating glass jar. When matching celestial orbs collide, they fuse into larger astronomical tiers—progressing through 11 tiers from microscopic Dust to supermassive Black Holes. Merging two Black Holes triggers a screen-clearing Supernova Ascension with permanent score multipliers. The game runs directly in modern web browsers with zero downloads, zero account requirements, and instant restart times.

What Is Stardust Merge?

Set inside a frosted glass containment jar drifting through deep space, Stardust Merge reinvents the tactile joy of arcade physics puzzles with a cozy, cosmic atmosphere and roguelite progression. Unlike static grid puzzles where tiles snap into rigid rows, Stardust Merge simulates real-time 2D rigid-body dynamics using Matter.js. Every dropped orb possesses mass, momentum, surface friction, and gravitational pull.

Starting from tiny specs of stardust and plasma sparks, every pair of matching bodies that collides fuses into the next astronomical tier. As your jar fills with glowing orbs, spatial management becomes paramount. Reaching score milestones unlocks stackable Cosmic Perks—such as magnetic attraction pulses and kinetic shockwaves—ensuring no two cosmic runs play out the same way.

How to Play Stardust Merge

Stardust Merge is intuitive for newcomers yet offers immense depth for high-score puzzle enthusiasts. Here is the core gameplay loop:

  • Aim & Drop: Drag your mouse, swipe your finger, or use the Left and Right arrow keys to position the laser guidance line and ghost preview orb along the top of the container. Release or press Space / Enter to drop the orb into the jar.
  • Merge Matching Tiers: When two identical celestial bodies collide, localized gravity pulls them together and fuses them into the next higher tier, releasing score points and kinetic impulse.
  • Use the Hold / Swap Slot: Inconvenient drop? Tap the Hold capsule in the top-left HUD (or press H) to store an orb in reserve or swap it with your active drop once per turn.
  • Watch the Event Horizon: A dashed red danger line marks the upper boundary of the containment vessel. If stacked orbs rest above this line continuously for 2.5 seconds (4.0 seconds with the Slow Orbit perk), containment collapses and the run ends.

Three Distinct Game Modes

Stardust Merge accommodates competitive strategists and casual players alike with three dedicated game modes:

  • Endless Odyssey: The classic endless arcade mode. Level up your score, draft stackable Cosmic Perks at milestones, and chase personal bests while surviving the Event Horizon.
  • Daily Challenge: A globally synchronized competitive challenge powered by a date-seeded mulberry32 pseudo-random number generator. Every player worldwide receives the exact same sequence of orbs, creating a level playing field for high-score prestige.
  • Zen Nebula: A peaceful, mindful puzzle mode with no Event Horizon barrier and no game-over screen. Stack cosmic orbs as high as you like accompanied by gentle generative ambient soundscapes.

Supernova Ascension: The Endgame Mechanic

In traditional drop-and-merge games, creating the largest body often leaves you stuck with an immovable barrier that ends your run. Stardust Merge turns endgame mastery into an exhilarating reward:

Uniting two Tier-11 Black Holes triggers a Supernova Ascension! Time dilates into slow motion as a radiant shockwave clears the entire glass vessel, granting +5,000 bonus points and permanently increasing your score multiplier by ×1.5. Your run continues seamlessly, allowing skilled players to stack multiple Supernovas on a single run.

Pro Strategy Tips for High Scores

  • Anchor Heavy Tiers in One Corner: Keep your largest bodies (Ocean World, Sun, Neutron Star) resting against one side wall. Arrange smaller tiers in descending order toward the opposite wall so incoming drops naturally roll downhill into matching partners.
  • Chain Within the 1.2s Combo Window: Consecutive merges completed within 1.2 seconds raise your multiplier up to ×8. When a cascade starts, drop your next queued orb promptly to sustain the multiplier.
  • Triage with Star Bombs: When tiny Dust or Spark orbs get trapped beneath giant planets and cannot merge, deploy your Star Bomb to instantly vaporize the five smallest orbs in the jar and lower your stack ceiling.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Stardust Merge?

Stardust Merge is a free, web-based cosmic physics merge puzzle game where players drop glowing orbs into a space jar, combining matching tiers from Dust to Black Hole to trigger Supernovas and unlock roguelite perks.

How do I play Stardust Merge?

Aim your drop by dragging with your mouse, swiping on touchscreens, or using the Left/Right arrow keys. Release to drop. When two orbs of the identical tier touch, they fuse into the next higher astronomical tier.

Is Stardust Merge free to play?

Yes, Stardust Merge is 100% free to play in any modern web browser with no download, no installation, and no paywalls.

Can I play Stardust Merge without downloading?

Yes. The game runs directly in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) using vanilla HTML5 Canvas and Matter.js. It loads in under one second on desktop and mobile.

Does Stardust Merge work on mobile?

Yes. Stardust Merge is fully responsive for smartphones and tablets, featuring touch-optimized drag-to-aim controls, bottom thumb buttons for dropping and nudging, and haptic feedback.

How does the orb merging system work?

When two bodies of the same tier collide, a localized gravitational pull draws them together into a fusion point. The parent orbs vanish and a body of the next tier spawns with dynamic kinetic momentum.

What happens when two Black Holes merge?

Merging two Tier-11 Black Holes triggers a Supernova Ascension! The container clears in a cinematic slow-motion shockwave, awarding +5,000 bonus points and permanently multiplying future score gains by ×1.5.

What is the Daily Challenge mode?

Daily Challenge mode uses a deterministic PRNG seeded with the calendar date (YYYYMMDD). Every player worldwide receives the identical sequence of incoming orbs for fair competitive comparison.

What is Zen Nebula mode?

Zen Nebula mode removes the Event Horizon game-over barrier completely, allowing players to relax, experiment with physics, and enjoy soothing generative audio without timer pressure.

How does the combo scoring system work?

Merges completed within a 1.2-second window (extendable to 2.2 seconds with the Combo Keeper perk) raise your active combo multiplier from ×1 up to ×8, accompanied by ascending musical chimes.

What are Cosmic Perks?

Cosmic Perks are roguelite run modifiers unlocked at score milestones (1,000, 3,000, 7,500 points). Options include Attractor Pulse, Kinetic Shockwaves, Slow Orbit, and Combo Keeper.

What does the Hold / Swap slot do?

The Hold slot in the top-left HUD allows you to store an inconvenient orb or swap it with your queued drop once per turn, providing essential tactical flexibility during tight situations.

What is the Event Horizon danger line?

The Event Horizon is the dashed boundary near the top of the jar. If stacked orbs rest above this line continuously for 2.5 seconds (or 4.0s with Slow Orbit), containment collapses and the run ends.

Does Stardust Merge save my progress?

Yes. All high scores, unlocked cosmetic skins, and audio preferences are saved locally and securely inside your browser's localStorage.

Does the game require an account or registration?

No. Stardust Merge has a strict zero-account, privacy-first design. You can start playing immediately without entering an email, password, or username.

Is Stardust Merge accessible for colorblind players?

Yes. Every celestial orb features a unique geometric surface pattern (dots, diamonds, craters, pulsar rings) in addition to color, and players can toggle on high-contrast numeral overlays (1–11) in Settings.
