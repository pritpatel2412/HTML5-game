What Is Stardust Merge?

Stardust Merge is a cozy cosmic physics puzzle game built directly for modern mobile and desktop web browsers. Set inside a frosted glass containment jar drifting through deep space, your mission is to synthesize increasingly massive celestial bodies. Starting from tiny specs of stardust and glowing plasma sparks, every pair of identical orbs that collides fuses together into the next astronomical tier. With realistic 2D rigid-body physics, momentum, and orbital cushions, every drop shifts the balance of the entire jar.

Unlike static grid puzzles, Stardust Merge blends tactile arcade physics with roguelite progression. Reaching score milestones unlocks stackable Cosmic Perks—from localized gravitational attraction to chain-reaction shockwaves—ensuring no two runs ever play out the same way. If you manage to unite two Tier-11 Black Holes at the pinnacle of the evolution ladder, you trigger a blinding Supernova Ascension, clearing the containment vessel, awarding a massive stardust bonus, and permanently multiplying all points earned for the remainder of your run.

How to Play Stardust Merge

  • Aim & Drop: Drag your finger or mouse horizontally across the top of the glass jar to position the laser guide line and ghost preview orb. Release to drop the orb into the jar. Keyboard players can also use Left / Right arrow keys and Space or Down to drop.
  • Merge Identical Tiers: Whenever two orbs of the exact same tier touch, they smoothly pull toward their midpoint and fuse into a single orb of the next higher tier, releasing a burst of kinetic energy.
  • Use the Hold / Swap Slot: Tap the Hold capsule in the top-left HUD to store an inconvenient orb for later or swap it with your currently queued drop.
  • Watch the Event Horizon: A dashed warning boundary spans the top of the jar. If stacked orbs rest above the Event Horizon continuously for 2.5 seconds (4.0 seconds with the Slow Orbit perk), the containment field destabilizes and the run ends.

Pro Tips & Cosmic Strategy Guide

Mastering high-score runs in Stardust Merge requires careful spatial ordering and combo timing:

  • Anchor Heavy Tiers on One Side: Keep your largest bodies (Ocean World, Ringed Giant, Sun, and Neutron Star) tucked against either the left or right wall, and arrange smaller tiers in descending order toward the opposite side so merges naturally cascade downhill.
  • Chain Within the 1.2-Second Combo Window: Consecutive merges completed within 1.2 seconds (or 2.2 seconds with Combo Keeper) raise your active multiplier up to ×8, accompanied by an ascending pentatonic musical scale.
  • Save Star Bombs for Tight Jams: When tiny Tier-1 Dust or Tier-2 Spark orbs wedge between two high-tier planets and block a merge, trigger your Star Bomb to vaporize the five smallest orbs inside the jar instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Stardust Merge require an account or download?

No. Stardust Merge runs natively inside your web browser with zero downloads, zero sign-ups, and instant one-tap restarts. All progress, achievements, and cosmetic unlocks are saved automatically in your browser's local storage.

What happens when two Black Holes merge?

Fusing two Tier-11 Black Holes triggers a Supernova Ascension! The entire jar clears in a slow-motion shockwave, awarding massive bonus points and increasing your permanent run score multiplier by ×1.5 while the cosmic ladder continues endlessly.

How does the Daily Challenge stay fair for everyone?

Daily Challenge mode initializes a deterministic mulberry32 pseudo-random number generator seeded by the current calendar date (YYYYMMDD). Every player worldwide receives the exact same sequence of upcoming orbs for that day.
