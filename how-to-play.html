How to Play Stardust Merge

The official guide to celestial fusion, scoring mathematics, and cosmic mastery.

1. The Core Objective

In Stardust Merge, you manage a cosmic containment jar. From above, you drop charming celestial bodies ranging in size from tiny specks of stardust to colossal ringed worlds. When two identical cosmic bodies collide, gravity fuses them into the next tier on the celestial ladder.

As orbs grow larger, space inside the jar becomes scarce. You must balance careful spatial stacking, momentum, and chain reactions to prevent orbs from crossing the dashed Event Horizon line near the top of the jar. Your crowning achievement is merging two Tier-11 Black Holes to trigger a cataclysmic Supernova Ascension!

4. Exact Scoring & Combo Mathematics

Points in Stardust Merge are calculated using the exact formula executed by the physics game engine:

Points = Math.round(Tier × Tier × 10 × Combo × AscensionMult)
  • Tier: The resulting tier created by the merge (Tier 2 to 11).
  • Combo: Starts at 1. Each rapid subsequent merge within 1.2 seconds increments this by +1 up to 8x!
  • AscensionMult: Starts at 1.0. Each Supernova cleared permanently multiplies this by 1.5x!

For instance, merging two Sun orbs (Tier 9) to form a Neutron Star (Tier 10) during a 4x combo at Ascension 1 yields:
10 × 10 × 10 × 4 × 1.0 = 4,000 points!

5. Cosmic Perks Catalog

Reaching score thresholds triggers the Mochi Milestone Perk Picker. You can choose one of 3 randomized perks to stack passive advantages:

  • GRAVITY CUDDLE: Dropped creatures exert a gentle gravitational pull on matching friends for 1.0 second upon landing.
  • SPARKLE CHAIN: 25% chance per stack for a merge to pull in nearby identical orbs and award +50% bonus points.
  • GOLDEN DUSTY: Tier-1 Dusty drops have a 35% chance per stack to upgrade into Tier-2 Sparky before falling.
  • PILLOW LANDING: Softens plush bounces (-45% restitution) for much steadier and safer stacking.
  • COMBO RIBBON: Extends your active combo window by +1.0 second per stack.
  • SAFE BUBBLE: Adds +1.5 seconds of Event Horizon grace time before game over is declared.
  • STAR POP +1: Grants an instant charge of the Star Bomb power-up, vaporizing the 5 smallest orbs in the jar.
  • MOCHI PEEK: Reveals two upcoming orbs in the Next queue and increases drop probabilities of higher tiers.

6. The 4 Game Modes

  • Endless Mode: The classic competitive mode. Climb the score ladder, unlock stacked perks, survive danger zones, and trigger endless Supernova Ascensions.
  • Daily Challenge: A globally synchronized daily competition. Every player receives the identical pseudo-random seed of drop orbs. Compete on the daily leaderboard!
  • Sleepy Zen Mode: A stress-free, meditative puzzle experience. Game over is disabled: whenever the jar gets full, Luna gently absorbs and vents excess dust.
  • Time Attack (90s): A rapid-fire 90-second adrenaline blitz! Merge as fast as you can to rack up massive combo multipliers before the clock hits zero.

7. 10 Pro Strategies to Dominate the Leaderboard

  1. Build a Descending Gradient: Keep your largest planets (Coco, Sunny, Saturny) anchored against the bottom corners. Arrange smaller tiers in descending order toward the other wall so merges cascade naturally downward.
  2. Avoid Trapping Small Dust Under Big Bodies: A tiny Dusty trapped under a massive Bubbly or Sunny creates an unstable “bubble gap” that destabilizes your entire jar.
  3. Watch the Next Queue Religiously: Always plan two moves ahead. If a heavy Tier 4 is coming, prepare a matching landing pad before it drops.
  4. Use the Hold Pocket Wisely: You can hold one orb for later. Save high-tier spawns until a compatible mate is ready in the jar.
  5. Master the Combo Window: When you see multiple potential merges, trigger the lowest merge first. The resulting motion often drops adjacent pairs into immediate cascade combos!
  6. Pillow Landing is a Lifesaver: If offered the Pillow Landing perk, take it! Bouncy orbs cause dangerous ricochets that trigger the Event Horizon prematurely.
  7. Reserve Star Bombs for Emergencies: Never waste a Star Bomb when the jar is empty. Save it for when orbs rise above 70% jar capacity to clear congestion.
  8. Shake Gently: The Shake button delivers a measured seismic jolt. Use it when two matching orbs are touching sides but stuck on an awkward shelf.
  9. Never Panic on the Red Line: When the jar glows red, you have 2.5 full seconds of grace time. Take a breath and find a merge to instantly collapse the pile.
  10. Prioritize Supernova: Getting two Black Holes together clears the entire jar and grants 5,000 × Ascension points. It is always worth sacrificing an upper tier to complete the Black Hole pair!

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the goal of Stardust Merge?

A: Drop cosmic orbs into the jar, merge matching tiers together, and manage jar space to build all the way to Coco the Black Hole and trigger Supernovas.

Q: How does the combo system calculate bonus points?

A: Merging within 1.2 seconds of a previous merge increases your multiplier by +1 up to 8x. Points are multiplied directly by this combo number.

Q: What happens when two Black Holes merge?

A: Merging two Tier-11 Black Holes creates a Supernova! The jar is completely cleared, awarding 5,000 points times your Ascension multiplier, and permanently increasing your score multiplier by 1.5x.

Q: How does the Event Horizon work?

A: If an orb crosses above the top dashed line and stays there for longer than the 2.5-second grace period, the game ends. Merging any orb resets the danger state immediately.

Q: Does Stardust Merge support Gamepads?

A: Yes! Connect any standard Bluetooth or USB controller (Xbox, PlayStation, or generic gamepad) and use the stick to aim and button A to drop.

Q: Can I play without internet?

A: Yes! Stardust Merge is 100% playable offline. Add the game to your home screen and play on airplanes, subways, or anywhere without cell reception.

Q: What is Daily Challenge mode?

A: In Daily Challenge, all players worldwide receive the exact same sequence of drop orbs based on the date. Your highest single run is recorded on the daily leaderboard.

Q: Can I transfer my save progress to another device?

A: Yes! Open Settings, tap “Export Save Code”, and copy your code. On your other device, open Settings and paste it into “Import Save Code”.
