About Stardust Merge

A handcrafted cosmic physics puzzle born from a passion for tactile, feel-good casual gaming.

What is Stardust Merge?

Stardust Merge is a free-to-play HTML5 physics puzzle game inspired by beloved drop-and-merge mechanics like Suika Game, blended with a playful cosmic theme. Players guide glowing celestial creatures—starting from humble specks of Dusty and playful bursts of Sparky, up through planetary titans like Sunny and Coco the Black Hole.

When two identical celestial bodies touch inside the jar, gravitational fusion draws them together, creating the next larger tier with an energetic pop, points, and cosmic coins. The ultimate challenge is managing jar space and achieving the legendary Supernova by merging two Black Holes!

Why It Was Made

In an era of hyper-commercialized mobile games cluttered with aggressive timers, paywalls, and cookie-cutter art, Stardust Merge was crafted to offer a breath of fresh air:

  • No Pay-to-Win Mechanics: Skill, spatial planning, and physics intuition determine your high score.
  • Original Cel-Shaded Sticker Aesthetic: Rejecting generic AI glow and dark neon trends, Stardust Merge embraces crisp 3px outlines, hard offset shadows, stepped lighting bands, and a locked 12-color harmonious palette.
  • True Accessibility & Offline Freedom: Built as a modern Progressive Web App (PWA), Stardust Merge works instantly on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktop monitors without downloading large app store packages. Once cached, the entire game functions in airplane mode.
  • Original Web Audio Synthesis: All chimes, drops, and celestial fanfare are synthesized live via the Web Audio API, keeping download weights tiny and performance snappy.

How We Fund the Project

Stardust Merge is completely free for everyone. To keep servers running and support continued development of new modes, seasonal events, and accessibility features, the game is supported by unobtrusive web advertising via Google AdSense.

We take player privacy and ad hygiene seriously:

  • We enforce strict distance gaps (at least 150 pixels) between game interactive canvases and ad banners to eliminate accidental clicks.
  • We strictly adhere to platform policies: standard display banners are disabled in standalone installed PWA mode where browser navigation is absent.
  • We collect no personal account data or login passwords on our servers.
  • You retain complete control over personalized versus non-personalized ad delivery via our in-site Cookie Settings.

Have Ideas or Feedback?

Stardust Merge thrives on player suggestions, bug reports, and community ideas. Whether you have ideas for new celestial tiers or found a glitch in the physics simulation, we would love to hear from you!
