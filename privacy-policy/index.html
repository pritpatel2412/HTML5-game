Privacy Policy

How Stardust Merge treats player data, browser local storage, and third-party advertising cookies.

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026
🛡️ Privacy at a Glance

Stardust Merge is committed to player privacy: We do not require user accounts, passwords, or personal registrations. All gameplay progress, high scores, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage. Third-party advertising (Google AdSense) uses standard cookies to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

1. Information We Store on Your Device (LocalStorage)

Stardust Merge stores game data exclusively on your device using the browser's localStorage API under the key stardust_merge_save_v1. This data includes:

  • High scores and total stardust coins earned.
  • Highest celestial tier reached (e.g. Tier 11 Black Hole).
  • Audio, music, haptic vibration, and accessibility display settings.
  • Unlocked cosmetic orb skins (e.g. Candy, Neon, Retro) and background themes.
  • Daily challenge completion status for the current calendar date.

This information never leaves your browser and is never sent to our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache and site data.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

To keep Stardust Merge 100% free for players worldwide, we partner with Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-8074288228358823) and Google's H5 Games Ads Placement API.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and California receive appropriate consent dialogues and options to opt out of personalized advertising.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through AboutAds.info.

3. Server Logs & Hosting

This website is statically hosted on GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub, Inc., a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). GitHub Pages may collect basic server log data such as visitor IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and access timestamps for security, DDoS protection, and service integrity.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Stardust Merge is a general-audience casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data held about you. Because Stardust Merge does not maintain user accounts or store personal identities on remote servers, your primary control is directly within your web browser (via clearing site storage and managing cookie preferences).

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance team via our Contact Page or by email at support@stardustmerge.example.com.
